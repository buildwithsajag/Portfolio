01 / Background

About Me

I'm an aspiring Agentic AI Engineer fascinated by the shift from static models to autonomous, tool-wielding agents. My background blends software development with a growing expertise in large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-step reasoning systems that can plan, act, and self-correct in the real world.

My journey into agentic AI began with a simple question: what happens when you give an LLM the ability to use tools and make decisions on its own? That curiosity led me to build prototype agents, experiment with orchestration frameworks, and study the emerging patterns that turn foundation models into reliable, goal-driven collaborators.
